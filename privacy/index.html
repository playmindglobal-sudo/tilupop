Privacy Policy — Tapita: Sort Puzzle

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Version: 1.0

This policy explains what data the game Tapita: Sort Puzzle uses, why, and what your rights are. It applies to:

  • the version that runs in a web browser (the game's website); and
  • the Android app (package com.tilupop.tapita), including test versions.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Renato Tavares Garcia, an individual, based in Presidente Prudente/SP. The game is published under the name Tilupop ("we", "us").

Contact and privacy channel: playmindglobal@gmail.com

Use this address for questions, requests and complaints about your data. Data protection authorities can also reach us there.

2. Summary

  • You do not create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • Ad measurement only with your permission. In the browser, if you tap Accept, we use the Meta Pixel to learn which ad brought you and how the game is played. If you tap Decline, the game works the same and this data is not sent.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can change your choice at any time under Privacy in the game menu.

3. What data we use

3.1 Data stored only on your device

The game keeps the following in your browser's or the app's local storage:

  • your current level and unlocked levels;
  • coins, win streak, best results and number of attempts;
  • whether sound is on;
  • your choice in the privacy notice;
  • the dates of your first and last visit, and the number of visits;
  • the parameters of the link that brought you (for example utm_content, which identifies the ad you clicked).

This data is not sent to us. To delete it, clear the site data in your browser or the app data in Android settings. This also deletes your progress.

3.2 Ad measurement with the Meta Pixel (browser, only if you accept)

If you tap Accept, the game loads the Meta Pixel. Meta is the company behind Facebook and Instagram. The game then sends Meta:

  • game events: game loaded, tutorial started and completed, level started and ended, level number, result (win, loss or quit), attempt number, reason for a loss, use of "continue", rewards received, interest in test store offers (nothing is ever charged), and returning on another day;
  • campaign data: the parameters of the ad link (utm_*).

The Pixel also sends Meta automatically:

  • your IP address;
  • browser and device information;
  • the page address;
  • Meta cookies (_fbp, and _fbc if you came from a Meta ad).

Meta may link this information to your Facebook or Instagram account and use it as described in the Meta Privacy Policy. We only see aggregated reports, such as "how many people who came from ad X reached level 5". We do not receive data that identifies you.

To see or control what Meta receives from websites and apps, use the "Your activity off Meta technologies" setting in your Meta account.

3.3 Server logs (browser)

For security and to keep the site running, the service that hosts the game website may log:

  • your IP address;
  • the date and time of the request;
  • the page requested;
  • your browser type.

These logs are technical. We do not use them to identify you.

3.4 Measurement tools in the Android app

In this version (closed test), the Android app uses no measurement tools, does not access the internet and sends no data, to us or to third parties. The whole game and your progress stay on your device. The tools below will only come in future versions, and this policy will be updated before that, with a request for consent where the law requires it. The app may use:

  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics): measures how the game is used and records crashes. It may process the same game events listed in 3.2, an app installation identifier, device model, Android version, language, approximate country (derived from the IP address) and crash reports.
  • Meta SDK: measures installs and which ad brought you. It may process the Android advertising ID, app events (install, app open and game events) and device information.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads). When one of these tools is turned on in the app, the Google Play "Data safety" section will show it.

3.5 When you contact us

If you write to us, we use your email address and your message only to reply.

3.6 Ads in the game

The game currently does not show third-party ads. The "ad" screens in the game are simulations and send no data. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

8. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you clear the site or app data.
  • At Meta: for the period set in Meta's policy and in the terms Meta applies to advertisers.
  • Aggregated reports: as long as we need them to make decisions about the game. They do not identify anyone.

  • Server logs: for the hosting provider's retention period.
  • Contact emails: as long as needed to resolve your request, plus any legal retention period.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can request:

  • confirmation that we process your data;
  • access;
  • correction;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of data that is unnecessary or unlawfully processed;
  • portability;
  • deletion of data processed on the basis of consent;
  • information about who we share data with;
  • information about what happens if you refuse consent;
  • withdrawal of consent.

If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR), you can also request:

  • access, rectification and erasure;
  • restriction of processing;
  • portability;
  • objection to processing;
  • withdrawal of consent.

How to request: write to playmindglobal@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days (LGPD art. 19) or within one month (GDPR).

Practical limit: we have no account or name for you, so we usually cannot link data to you. Tell us which device and browser you used and we will do our best. You can view and delete the data Meta holds through the tools in your Meta account.

Complaints: you can complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Agency (ANPD) at gov.br/anpd, or to the data protection authority in your EU country or the UK.
